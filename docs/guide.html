Back up your stats to your own Google Sheet

A one-off, 10-minute setup. Your darts stats are saved to a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive, and every device you use can sync with it.

Before you start

  • You need a Google account (the one you use for Gmail is fine).
  • Do this on a computer. Google's script editor is hard to use on a phone. Once it is set up, you can connect your phone in seconds with the QR code (step 12).
  • Keep TapDarts open in another tab. You will copy a script from it and paste a link back into it.

1Create a new Google Sheet

Go to sheets.new to make a blank spreadsheet. Click the title at the top left (it says Untitled spreadsheet) and give it a name, such as My Darts.

2Open Apps Script

In the menu, click Extensions, then Apps Script. A new tab opens with Google's script editor.

3Paste the TapDarts script and save

  1. In TapDarts, go to Setup and press Copy script.
  2. Back in Apps Script, click in the code area, select everything that is already there (Ctrl+A, or ⌘A on a Mac) and delete it.
  3. Paste the TapDarts script (Ctrl+V or ⌘V).
  4. Click the save icon (a disk). Unsaved changes at the top changes to Saved to Drive.
Paste over everything. If any of Google's sample code (function myFunction() {}) is left behind, the script will not work.

4Start a new deployment

At the top right, click the blue Deploy button, then New deployment.

5Choose "Web app"

In the window that opens, click the gear icon next to Select type, then choose Web app.

6Set who can use it, then Deploy

  • Description: anything you like, such as TapDarts.
  • Execute as: Me (it shows your email address).
  • Who has access: Anyone. This is the important one.

Then click Deploy at the bottom right.

If Who has access is anything other than Anyone, TapDarts cannot reach your Sheet and Sync will fail.

7Authorise access

Google asks you to allow the script to use your Sheet. Click Authorise access, then pick your Google account if asked.

8Get past "Google hasn't verified this app"

This warning is normal. The "app" is the script you just pasted into your own Sheet, and Google shows this for any script that hasn't been through its review.

  1. Click Advanced (small, at the bottom left).
  2. Click Go to Untitled project (unsafe).
The script only reads and writes this one spreadsheet, and only you can change it. If you named your Apps Script project, you will see that name instead of "Untitled project".

9Allow

Google lists what the script can do (see and edit your spreadsheets). Scroll down if needed and click Allow. Some accounts show tick boxes first: tick them all, then Continue.

10Copy the Web app URL

You will see Deployment successfully updated. Under Web app, click Copy below the URL. It starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/ and ends with /exec. Then click Done.

Treat this link like a password. Anyone who has it can read your darts data and add rows to your Sheet.

11Paste it into TapDarts and sync

In TapDarts, go to Setup, paste the link into the URL box, press Save, then Sync now. You should see "Synced." and your Sheet gets its worksheets (Visits, Mickey Mouse, Killer and Footy).

That's it. TapDarts syncs on its own after each game. Press Sync now any time to pull in games from your other devices.

12Add your phone or another device

On the device that is already set up, go to Setup and press Show QR code. Scan it with the other device's camera. TapDarts opens there with your Sheet already connected and syncs straight away.

Or do it by hand: paste the same link into Setup on the other device, then Save and Sync now.

When TapDarts asks you to update the script

Sometimes a new version of TapDarts needs a newer script. Your link stays the same:

  1. Copy the script again from TapDarts Setup, paste it over the old one in Apps Script, and save.
  2. Click Deploy, then Manage deployments.
  3. Click the pencil (Edit) icon, set Version to New version, and click Deploy.

If Sync doesn't work

  • "The URL returned a web page, not data": Who has access isn't set to Anyone (step 6), or the link isn't the one ending in /exec. Fix the setting, deploy a new version (see above) and try again.
  • "The script has no doGet/doPost": some of Google's sample code was left behind, or the paste was cut short. Redo step 3, then deploy a new version.
  • "Could not reach the URL": check you are online and that you pasted the whole link.
  • Nothing from your other device? Press Sync now on that device first, so its games reach the Sheet, then Sync here.
Your stats live in your own Google Drive, not on a TapDarts server.
